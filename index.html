ABOUT SLMS

A centralized academic management system.

The Student Learning Management System is designed to help schools efficiently manage learning activities and academic information through one centralized system.

Administrators can manage users, courses, subjects, classes, learning materials, announcements, enrollment, academic records, reports, settings, and activity logs.
